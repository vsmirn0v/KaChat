KaChat Wallet Privacy Policy

The KaChat Wallet browser extension (Chrome, Brave, Edge, Firefox and other browsers)

Company: Cyber Ventures LLC

Website: https://kachat.app | Support: support@kachat.app

Effective date: September 30, 2026

KaChat Wallet is a self-custody Kaspa wallet. Cyber Ventures LLC does not run an account system for it, does not collect personal data from it, and has no analytics, tracking or advertising in it. The extension never sends anything to Cyber Ventures LLC.

1. What stays on your computer

  • Recovery phrases and passphrases are stored only in your browser, encrypted with your wallet password (PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 rounds, then AES-256-GCM). They are never sent anywhere. The password itself is never stored.
  • While the wallet is unlocked, the decryption key is kept in the browser's session memory, which is cleared when the wallet locks (automatically after a period you choose) or when the browser closes.
  • Account names, derived public addresses, settings, the websites you have connected, and cached public data (balances, KNS domain names, prices) are stored in the extension's local storage in your browser.

2. What the extension sends to the network, and why

To show balances and send transactions, the wallet talks directly to public services. These requests include your public Kaspa addresses, which are already visible to everyone on the Kaspa blockchain. They never include your recovery phrase, passphrase, password or private keys.

  • Kaspa network nodes (public nodes found through the Kaspa resolver, or a node you choose in Settings): balances, coins, and submitting the transactions you approve.
  • Kaspa REST API (api.kaspa.org by default, or one you set): transaction history and whether an address has been used.
  • KNS API (api.knsdomains.org): the .kas domain names your address owns, name lookups when you type a name, and the signed request when you set a primary domain.
  • dotk (api.dotk.name) and Kaspa Names (kaspaname.com): the .k and .kaspa names your address owns, and name lookups when you type a name. KaChat Wallet only reads these services; it never registers names with them.
  • CoinGecko (api.coingecko.com): the Kaspa price in your chosen currency. No address is sent.
  • Block explorer (explorer.kaspa.org or kaspa.stream): only when you open a "view in explorer" link.

These services are run by third parties and have their own privacy practices. Like any web request, they can see your IP address.

3. Websites you connect

Kaspa websites can ask to connect to KaChat Wallet. Nothing is shared until you approve the connection in the wallet. A connected website can see the chatting address, public key and balance of the account you chose, and can ask you to send Kaspa or sign a message. Every such request opens KaChat Wallet and needs your approval. You can disconnect any website in Settings > Connected Sites.

4. Permissions

  • storage: keeps the encrypted wallet and your settings in your browser.
  • alarms: locks the wallet after the auto-lock time.
  • Access to the Kaspa, name service and CoinGecko addresses: the network requests listed above.
  • Access to websites: provides the window.kachat connection that Kaspa websites use to ask for your approval. The extension does not read, collect or change website content.
  • Optional access to other https addresses: requested only if you set your own Kaspa REST API in Settings.

5. Removing your data

Remove an account from the accounts screen, reset the wallet from the unlock screen, or uninstall the extension to delete everything it stored in your browser. Your Kaspa stays on the blockchain and can be restored with your recovery phrase.

6. Children

KaChat Wallet is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
